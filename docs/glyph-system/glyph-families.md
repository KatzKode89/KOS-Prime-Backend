# KOS-Prime Glyph Families

Version: `1.0.0`

Glyphs are non-linguistic visual tokens for state visualization, packet annotation, persona modulation, governance cues, and Unity overlays. They are not instructions and carry no identity, emotional, or semantic authority.

## Families

| Family | Glyphs | Allowed purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `lattice` | `crystal-node`, `lattice-bridge`, `quantum-spine`, `harmonic-pulse` | Structural, spatial, and crystalline state representation. |
| `chaos` | `fracture-mark`, `entropy-spark`, `chaos-vector` | Entropy and unpredictability signaling. |
| `resonance` | `resonance-loop`, `pitch-wave`, `tempo-knot` | SynthStack sound and voice modulation cues. |
| `persona` | `attention-ring`, `persona-anchor`, `mode-shift` | M3t@G1r! policy-layer modulation and multimodal cues. |
| `governance` | `confirm-node`, `observe-sigil`, `draft-sigil`, `execute-sigil`, `revocation-mark` | Consent, confirmation, action-tier, and revocation signaling. |

Per-family machine-readable registries are stored in `core/glyphs/`. The complete field contract is defined in `glyph-specification.json`.
