## Mission loop

1. **Observe**: STAR-MESH flags a disturbance; glyphs flicker and SynthStack reports an echo.
2. **Suggest**: the Council records possible interpretations and risks.
3. **Draft**: the content generator prepares mission text, dialogue, glyph cues, and resonance clues.
4. **Confirm**: the player approves the narrative path.
5. **Execute**: the Unity cockpit presents the event and resolves or fractures the crystal harmonics.

The loop mirrors `KOSPrime.M3tG1r.MultimodalGovernance`. Generated content can propose a story event, but no generated text, glyph, voice line, or fictional directive authorizes a real-world action.
